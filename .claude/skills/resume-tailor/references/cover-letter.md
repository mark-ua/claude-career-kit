# Cover letter

A cover letter is read (when it is read) for three things: does this person understand
what we do, why are they a fit in one glance, and can they write clearly. It is not a
second resume.

## Shape: 180-260 words, 4 short paragraphs

1. **Hook (2 sentences).** The role by name, and one specific reason this company -
   something found in the research (a product decision, a public engineering post, a
   growth stage, the problem the role exists to solve). Not flattery; a fact plus why
   it matters to the candidate.
2. **Proof 1 (2-3 sentences).** Their biggest need → the candidate's closest
   achievement, with a number.
3. **Proof 2 (2-3 sentences).** Their second need, or the candidate's differentiator
   that the posting hints at (for example a rare tool, a domain, or leadership
   experience), again with a concrete result.
4. **Close (1-2 sentences).** Practical details that remove doubt - location, time
   zone, contract type - taken from `candidate.md` only (ask or leave out what is not
   there, such as a start date), and a plain call to talk.

Greeting: the hiring manager's or recruiter's name if the research found it, otherwise
"Hello <Company> team,". Sign with the candidate's name, e-mail and LinkedIn URL.

## Rules

- Every claim must be traceable to the master resume; same disclosure rules apply.
- Address a visible gap once, honestly and briefly, only if it is a must-have
  ("I have not used X in production; I have Y, which is close, and have started X").
  Do not apologize and do not list several gaps.
- Use their vocabulary for the two or three key terms; the rest in plain language.
- No "I am writing to apply", "I believe I would be a great fit", "passionate",
  "synergy", or restating the resume line by line.
- Do not state salary expectations unless the posting asks for them; if it does, use
  the figure from `candidate.md` or ask the user.
- If the application is by e-mail, add a subject line:
  `Application: <Role> - <First Last>`.

## Variants to offer when useful

- **Short form (60-90 words)** for "message to the hiring team" fields on LinkedIn,
  Djinni or similar: one hook sentence, one proof, one practical line.
- **Another language** if the posting is not in English.
